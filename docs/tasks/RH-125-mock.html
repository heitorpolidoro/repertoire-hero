RH-125 — a playlist entry is a version

After this task a setlist row names a recording, not a song. Two takes of the same song can sit in the same setlist, and the key/tuning/lyrics shown on a row are resolved from (this playlist's owner, that version). One row type is new and needs a decision: a song that is in the setlist but not in the owner's repertoire.

The setlist, with two takes of one song

The version label is the suffix after the title. Both rows below are "Enter Sandman" by Metallica — the album take and the live take — and each carries its own key.

Page question — a song in the setlist that is not in the repertoire

This happens when a song is removed from the repertoire but left in the setlist. It is not an error: the row stays, showing its title, artist, version label and duration. The question is what its mastery chip looks like, since there is no personal progress to show and nothing to cycle when it is tapped.

A Exactly like today: a normal "Unknown" chip

Looks identical to a song you own and have not started. Tapping it does nothing, silently.

B A muted "Not in repertoire" chip, not tappable

Says why there is no progress, and does not invite a tap that cannot do anything. The spec currently picks B.

Resolution, for context (not a question)

The same version shown in a personal playlist and in a band playlist is read against two different rows. Toggle the owner and watch the mastery chip change — nothing about the entry itself changes, only whose repertoire row the chip comes from. (The row shows no key: the playlist list draws title, artist, version label, duration and the chip, and nothing else.)
