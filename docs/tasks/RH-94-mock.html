RH-94 - Formatting guide beside the lyrics editor

Fast View, Lyrics section, after pressing Edit. Each guide entry shows the text to type and its preview, rendered with the same four rules the parser uses. Edit the text and press Save to see it rendered on the lyrics card, or Cancel: outside edit mode the guide disappears. Edit brings it back.

Wide screen (sm and up) - guide beside the textarea

Auto-import, Cancel and Save sit directly under the textarea and end where the textarea ends; nothing runs under the guide column. The guide is always open here (no "Formatting help" line).

Phone placement - option B (chosen)

Under the textarea there is one "Formatting help" line, closed by default. Tapping it shows the four entries as a 2x2 grid between the textarea and the buttons; tapping again hides them. Both phones are live - the left one starts closed, the right one starts open.
