Setlist Player: Previous Click Behavior

Threshold: 3.0s

Standard media player convention: clicking Previous (⏮) after > 3 seconds of playback restarts the current song from 0:00 without decrementing queue index and without triggering countdown. Clicking within the first ≤ 3 seconds steps back to the prior song with countdown.

  • Elapsed time counts only while playing (pause stops the clock).
  • Restart while playing: seek to 0:00 only — playback just continues (no new play call).
  • Restart while paused: seek to 0:00, then resume playback once.
  • On the first song (1 / N): Previous is enabled while the song is playing or paused, and always restarts it.
  • Previous is disabled at 1 / N only while counting down, stopped, or offline.
