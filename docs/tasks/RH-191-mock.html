    2. Your decisions

    • Removing one song: B. Every song row carries a mark and, when the song is stored, a trash button, as in section 1. A song not on the device has only the dashed mark.
    • Removing a setlist: delete. Its songs are deleted, except those in another downloaded setlist or downloaded on their own. Section 1 behaves this way.
