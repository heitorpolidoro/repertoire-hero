1. Live flow (option A, the spec's pick)

Personal mode: a slim bar at the top of the page that scrolls away with the content. Band mode: the RH-180 purple bar, where the band name is now a button. Exit works as before.

What to try

  • Tap Switch ▾. A sheet opens above the bottom nav, listing Personal and each band. The active one is ticked.
  • Pick a band. The heading becomes “🎸 band name” and the purple bar appears.
  • Tap the band name in the purple bar to switch again, or tap Exit.
  • Tap the dark backdrop, Close, or press Esc. Nothing changes.

With no bands at all, the personal bar is not shown, because there is nothing to switch to.
