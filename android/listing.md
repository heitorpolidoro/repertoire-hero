## Short description

Your songs, setlists and band repertoire, ready for the stage, even offline.

## Full description

Repertoire Hero keeps every song you play in one place, so rehearsal and gigs stop depending on scattered folders, chat links and paper.

- Build your repertoire: catalog songs with key, tuning, lyrics and links, and track how well you know each one, from unknown to mastered.
- Take it offline: download a playlist before the gig and read lyrics, keys, statuses and PDF charts with no signal at all.
- Fast View and Stage Mode: a reading mode made for a music stand, with PDF tablature you can write on with a finger or a pen.
- Setlists: move between the songs of a playlist with a swipe.
- Bands: share a repertoire and playlists with your band, with an invite link and a band repertoire of its own.
- Spotify: search tracks and import whole playlists.
